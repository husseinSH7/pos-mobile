## Long Description (4000 characters)

Restaurant POS is a complete point-of-sale system designed for restaurants, cafes, and food service businesses. Manage orders, tables, kitchen operations, and payments all from your mobile device.

### Key Features:
- **Order Management**: Create and manage dine-in, takeout, and delivery orders
- **Table Management**: Visual table layout with real-time status updates
- **Kitchen Display**: Kitchen ticket system with order status tracking
- **Payment Processing**: Support for cash, card, and split payments
- **Offline Mode**: Continue working even without internet connection
- **Real-time Sync**: Automatic synchronization when connection is restored
- **Staff Management**: PIN-based login for different staff roles
- **Customer Management**: Track customer information and loyalty
- **Shift Management**: Track staff shifts and performance
- **Inventory Tracking**: Monitor product availability and stock levels
- **Reservation System**: Manage table reservations and waitlist

### Perfect For:

- Fine dining establishments
